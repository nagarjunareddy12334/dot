# ✨ Constellation Cinema | Romantic Love Story Film

A cinematic web application that transforms a pitch-black screen into a multi-scene love story animated in glowing celestial white dots and starlight.

## 🎬 The 3 Romantic Chapters
1. **Act 1: The Night Walk (Walking Scene)**
   - Couple walking hand-in-hand along a romantic tree-lined pathway illuminated by glowing street lanterns and fairy lights.
   - Twinkling lantern flares and gentle drifting fireflies.
   - *Caption: "Walking by your side is where my heart belongs ✨"*

2. **Act 2: Candlelight Table Date (Dinner Date Scene)**
   - Romantic dinner table for two: holding hands across the table, warm glowing candle flames with rising ember particles, wine glasses, and a fresh rose.
   - Animated rising golden embers and warm candle flicker physics.
   - *Caption: "In the warm glow of candlelight, time stands still with you 🕯️🍷"*

3. **Act 3: Her Eternal Constellation (Her Portrait Reveal)**
   - Begins with a pulsating celestial heart formed out of stardust that beats to an acoustic heartbeat audio.
   - Blossoms outward into her radiant portrait with dazzling diamond cross-star sparkles (`✦`) on her jhumka earrings, necklace, and smile!
   - *Caption: "You are the brightest star in my universe ❤️"*

## 🌟 Key Features
- **🎬 Full Story Film Mode**: Plays continuously through all 3 acts with smooth cinematic transitions (~33s total).
- **🌙 Chapter Navigation Bar**: Jump directly to any scene (*Night Walk*, *Table Date*, or *Her Portrait*).
- **📹 Record & Export Full Movie**: 1-click button to record the full 60 FPS HD short film directly to your Downloads!
- **Built-in Ambient Soundscape**: Pure Web Audio API romantic piano, celesta, and heartbeat synthesizer.
- **Interactive Stardust Waves**: Touch or move mouse over any scene to create ripples in the constellation stars.
- **Offline & Zero CORS**: All assets pre-encoded in base64 for instant zero-dependency double-click execution.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally
Double-click `Start Cinema.bat` or open `index.html` in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave).
