# 💖 Our Love Story | 5-Act Constellation Cinema

A personal, deeply romantic 5-act love story film that transforms a pitch-black screen into glowing celestial white-dot constellation memories.

---

## 🎬 The 5 Chapters of Our Love Story

### 💖 Act 1: Love Symbol & Her Portrait
* Starts in the dark cosmos as thousands of white stardust particles swarm into a **pulsating celestial love heart** that beats in rhythm with the music.
* The heart blossoms outward into **her radiant smiling portrait**, accented by sparkling diamond cross-star flares (`✦`) on her jhumka earrings and necklace!
* *Caption: "Every heartbeat in this universe beats for you ❤️"*

### 👻 Act 2: A Random Snap Request (How We Met)
* A glowing smartphone in deep space showing that fateful **Snapchat friend request notification**, with stardust streaming into twin intertwined celestial hearts.
* *Caption: "It all began with one random Snapchat request... and turned into my whole world 👻💬✨"*

### 🌙 Act 3: Falling In Love (Endless Walks)
* Walking hand-in-hand together along a romantic tree-lined pathway lit by lanterns and fairy lights, with gentle floating fireflies.
* *Caption: "Late night talks became endless walks, and day by day, I fell deeply in love with you 🌙✨"*

### 🛕 Act 4: Our Sacred Temple Date
* Ancient stone temple illuminated by rows of warm brass oil lamps (diyas) with gentle flickering flame particles, seeking eternal blessings together.
* *Caption: "Holding hands before God, praying for a lifetime of love and togetherness 🛕🪔🙏"*

### 🕯️ Act 5: Candlelight Date & Forever (My Gift To You)
* Intimate candlelight dinner: holding hands across the table, warm glowing candles with rising golden embers, wine glasses, and a red rose.
* *Caption: "To my favorite person, my greatest love... this is my forever gift to you 🕯️🍷💖"*

---

## 🌟 Features
* **🎬 Continuous Film Playback**: Plays smoothly through all 5 acts with cinematic transitions (~53s total).
* **Chapter Tabs**: Jump directly to any memory anytime (*Her Portrait*, *Snap Request*, *Night Walk*, *Temple Date*, *Candlelight Date*).
* **📹 Record & Export Video**: 1-click button to record the full 60 FPS HD film directly to your Downloads folder!
* **Built-in Romantic Audio**: Ambient piano, celesta, and heartbeat synthesizer generated live with Web Audio API.
* **Interactive Stars**: Touch or move mouse to create water-wave ripples in the stardust.
* **100% Offline & Zero Setup**: All 5 scenes are pre-encoded in base64. Just double-click `Start Cinema.bat` or open `index.html`!

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally
Double-click `Start Cinema.bat` or open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Brave.
